## Summary Content

SI MINERAL WATER GRADUATION TOWER

Inside the graduation tower/pavilion, whick boasts a mineral water fountain and a salt gallery. mineral water flows down the evaporator walls evaporating naturally from the impact of sun and wind. Healthy ‘sea’ air enriched with minerals can then be inhaled in the tower itself and within a 50-80 metre radius of it.

Air enriched with drops of mineral water has the same effect as breathing sea air. Breathing air saturated with salts is not only beneficial for people with various respiratory diseases - it also has a positive effect on the skin, which becomes smoother, brighter and more elastic from the mineral water. The meditation of watching the rippling water also calms the nerves and improves one’s sense of well-being. Springs of water spurting from the depths of the earth can be used for

inhalation or sensory therapy during the warm and cold seasons.

The sea air-that the evaporator emits allows the rehabilitation procedures carried out at BirStonas’ health resorts to be fully coordinated with wellness through natural factors, which will enhance the effects of

treatment and permit patients to more quickly regain their working capacity. ae
