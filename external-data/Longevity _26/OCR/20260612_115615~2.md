Barrier Healing Anticancer Immunosurveillance

> Chronic wounds (diabetes, venous insufficiency) represent failure of this containment. > Immunosenescence reduces surveillance > age-dependent cancer incidence rise.

Delimitation of Foreign Bodies Cellular Senescence & Its Clearance

Granuloma formation encapsulates foreign material (bacteria, inorganic particles) and surveillance. Accumulation = senescent cell burden.

> Tuberculosis granuloma: successful containment vs. immune failure > systemic spread. > Senolytic therapy (dasatinib + quercetin, navitoclax) restores containment capacity.

Self-Limited Inflammation Containment of Other Perturbations

Acute inflammation must resolve via pro-resolving lipid mediators (resolvins, protectins), Includes epigenetic containment (heterochromatin stability), metabolic regulatory T cells, and IL-10 signaling. compartmentalization, and organelle stress signaling.

> Epigenetic drift with aging > loss of chromatin domains > transposable element activation.

> Failure > chronic low-grade inflammation ('inflammaging‘) driving all age-related diseases.

Innate & Acquired Immune Responses

Compartmentalized immune activation — local lymph node priming, IgA secretion at mucosae, tissue-resident macrophages — contains pathogens without systemic activation.

> Systemic immune activation (sepsis) = catastrophic failure of containment.
