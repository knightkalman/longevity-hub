## 1. Overview & Core Themes

The Longevity '26 conference brought together clinicians, geroscience researchers, digital health innovators, and health economists. The presentations centered around the transition from reactive sick-care to proactive, precision-guided longevity medicine.

The proceedings divide into five major thematic pillars:

1. **Biomarkers & Biological Age Clocks** (suPAR, hsCRP, Redox GSH/GSSG ratios, multi-omics).
2. **AI & Digital Twins** (multi-scale multi-omics integration for disease trajectory modeling).
3. **Lifestyle, Nutrition & Cognitive Health** (FINGER protocol, plant-predominant metabolic nutrition).
4. **Cellular Hallmarks & Stress Biology** (hormesis, DNA repair, mitochondrial resilience, autophagy/mTOR).
5. **Clinical Infrastructure & Health Policy** (reimbursement models, modern longevity clinics, medical spa/balneotherapy).

## 2. Day-by-Day Session Breakdown

### Day 1: June 9, 2026 — Inflammation, AI Models & Digital Health
- **Chronic Inflammation & Biomarkers**:
  - Comparison of standard acute markers (e.g., `hsCRP`) vs. stable cellular immune activation markers (`suPAR` — soluble urokinase plasminogen activator receptor).
  - Demonstration of longitudinal biological age tracking via `suPAR` (optimal target < 2.0 ng/ml; correlating rate of aging < 1.0 with lowered disease incidence).
- **AI & Multi-Scale Digital Twins**:
  - Construction of virtual patient models integrating genomics (DNA), transcriptomics (RNA), proteomics, and clinical EHR data.
  - Al-guided therapy optimization and biological simulation for personalized anti-aging interventions.
- **Nutritional Biochemistry & Longevity Pathways**:
  - Caloric and macronutrient modulation: 45–60% unrefined complex carbohydrates, 25–35% predominantly plant fats, 10–15% protein (reducing methionine/BCAA overactivation).
  - Suppression of pro-aging pathways (mTOR, IGF-1, hyperinsulinemia) and stimulation of pro-longevity mechanisms (autophagy, AMPK).
- **Digital Health & Assistive Living**:
  - Wearable sensors, remote continuous monitoring, AI companions (ElliQ), and smart ambient sensors reducing elderly fall risks by up to 80%.
  - Overview of Israeli geroscience ecosystem and global translational health initiatives (Prof. Richard Barker, Oxford).
- **Cardiometabolic Continuum**:
  - Diagnostic criteria and staging for Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) syndrome.

---

### Day 2: June 11, 2026 — Body Composition, Genetics & Redox Diagnostics
- **Segmental Body Composition & Sarcopenia**:
  - Multi-frequency bioimpedance assessment (Tanita medical analyzers): segmental lean muscle distribution (arms, legs, trunk) vs. visceral adipose tissue.
  - Sarcopenic obesity identification as an underdiagnosed driver of metabolic decline.
- **Oxidative Stress & Redox Profiling**:
  - Clinical significance of glutathione balance: reduced (`GSH`) vs. oxidized (`GSSG`) glutathione, alongside gamma-glutamyl transferase (`GGT`).
- **Genetics vs. Epigenetics in Practice**:
  - Limits of single-gene predictive power vs. actionable epigenetic and lifestyle modifications.
  - Call for standardized medical data sharing and health data interoperability.

---

### Day 3: June 12, 2026 — Public Health, Hallmarks of Aging & Cognitive Protection
- **Longevity Economics**:
  - Shift from viewing healthcare as an unsustainable cost to treating healthy longevity as a macroeconomic growth driver (Lancet Healthy Longevity framework).
- **National Preventative Strategy (Lithuanian & Baltic Health Initiatives)**:
  - Targeted epidemiological interventions for common age-related morbidities: hearing impairment, depression, air pollution, and cardiovascular risk reduction.
- **Multi-Domain Cognitive Preservation (The FINGER Model)**:
  - Application of the Finnish Geriatric Intervention Study: simultaneous synergy of Mediterranean-style diet, resistance/aerobic exercise, cognitive brain training, and metabolic risk management.
- **Deep Biology of Aging**:
  - Three interlocking stress response strategies: homeostatic regulation, hormesis (adaptive mild stress), and structural repair.
  - Cellular renewal cycles: apoptosis, senolysis, and stem cell replacement.
  - Comparative biology insights into long-lived species: enhanced double-strand DNA repair, mitochondrial fidelity, and proteostatic maintenance.

### Day 4: June 13, 2026 — Longevity Clinics, Prevention Policy & Integrative Therapies
- **Clinical Operational Models**:
  - PRIMA Longevity Policy Institute guidelines for institutionalizing longevity medicine.
  - Slovakia's national insurance-reimbursed medical obesity management program as a template for preventative care funding.
- **Validated Multi-Marker Ratio Panels**:
  - Evaluation of 6 key clinical biomarker ratios across independent clinical trials (inflammatory, metabolic, lipid, and redox indicators).
- **Evidence-Based Balneotherapy & Medical Spa Interventions**:
  - Clinical evidence for mineral balneotherapy, thermal muds, and structured hydrotherapy.
  - Physiological modulation of the Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Adrenal (HPA) axis, cortisol reduction, systemic inflammation dampening, and functional mobility improvement.
- **Academic Horizons**:
  - Announcement of the Bar-Ilan University International Longevity Conference (November 23–24, 2026).

## 3. Key Takeaways for Practice & Research

| Focus Area | Core Insight | Key Biomarkers / Tools |
|------------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Immune / Inflammation** | Low-grade systemic inflammation is the primary driver of multi-morbidity. | `suPAR`, `hsCRP`, CKM staging |
| **Metabolism & Diet** | Moderate protein with low methionine/BCAAs and high fiber suppresses mTOR and activates autophagy. | Glucose variability, visceral fat score, lipid subfractions |
| **Cellular Resilience** | Hormesis and active DNA repair preserve cellular function longer than passive protection. | Redox ratios (`GSH`/`GSSG`), mitochondrial markers |
| **Cognitive Decline** | Single-factor interventions fail; multi-domain lifestyle synergy is mandatory. | FINGER trial protocol, neuromodulation |
| **Healthcare Policy** | Preventative programs must be formalized and integrated into public/private reimbursement. | Longevity clinic accreditation, national prevention registries |
