## Summary Content

What Makes a Valid Hallmark of Health?

Three requisites that each hallmark must fulfill — Löpez-Otin et al., Cell 2021

Associated with the Healthy State

The characteristic must be robustly present in healthy organisms and quantifiably reduced or absent in diseased states. It must be measurable across the life span and

across species.

Perturbation is Vastly Pathogenic

Experimental disruption — or naturally occurring mutations — that compromise this hallmark must lead to clear and reproducible disease phenotypes. The damage must be

dose-dependent and mechanistically explicable.

Maintenance or Restoration is Pro-Health

Interventions that preserve, restore or enhance the hallmark — pharmacological, lifestyle, or genetic — must demonstrably improve health outcomes or extend healthy lifespan in model organisms and ideally in humans.

These criteria mirror the Koch postulates for infectious disease — a rigorous causal standard applied to the biology of health.
