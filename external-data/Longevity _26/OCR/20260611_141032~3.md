Transposon Theory of Aging — Evidence Base

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

Cross-species correlation Species with longer lifespan have proportionally more efficient TE silencing mechanisms and smaller total transposon loads relative to genome size.

Somatic LINE-1, Alu, and HERV transcription increases measurably with age in blood, brain, and intestinal tissue in multiple model Organisms and humans.

Epigenetic drift is the trigger face easiv loss of H2XSme3, DNA methylation, and HOAC activity at TE loci correlates directly with rising TE expression — the same changes driven by dysbiosis.

IS AGING A VIRAL DISEASE?

Emerging anti-TE interventions:

Nucleoside RT inhibitors Lamivudine (3TC), tenofovir — repurposed HIV antiretrovirals that suppress LINE-1 reverse transcriptase. Human trials underway.

DNA MT activators / methyl donors Restore DNA methylation at TE loci, Folate, SAM, betaine — directly

relevant to microbiome axis interventions.

piRNA pathway support Indirect — compounds Supporting PIWI-interacting RNA production to re-silence germline and somatic TEs,
