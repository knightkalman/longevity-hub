Landmark Discoveries

The Geroscience Trifecta.

Three paradigm-shifting scientific pillars presented at Longevity '26 that represent the modern vanguard of lifespan medicine.

Biomarker Breakthrough 01

The suPAR Revolution in Biological Clocks

While conventional hsCRP spikes with transient infections or minor stress, soluble urokinase plasminogen activator receptor (suPAR) remains constant, reflecting deep cellular immune senescence, vascular vulnerability, and true rate of aging.

Clinical Threshold: A suPAR score below 1.8 ng/ml correlates with a biological rate of aging < 0.87, moving individuals out of chronic multi-morbidity risk zones into longevity phenotypes.
Immune Aging & Diagnostics Session 1 · Slides 1–5
Predictive Intelligence 02

Multi-Scale Digital Twins: Simulate Before Disease

Instead of treating isolated diseases as they manifest, modern AI combines genomics (DNA), transcriptomics (RNA), proteomics, and continuous telemetry into Virtual Patient Digital Twins capable of testing interventions in silico.

Predictive Power: Al algorithms simulate drug responses, metabolic fasting cycles, and lifestyle changes to select optimal individualized longevity protocols before clinical trial.
Artificial Intelligence Session 1 · Slides 6–7
Cognitive Protection 03

The FINGER Synergy: Multi-Domain Brain Resilience

Data from the landmark Finnish Geriatric Intervention Study confirms that single-factor interventions (monotherapies or isolated supplements) fail to prevent neurodegeneration. Brain preservation demands simultaneous, coordinated synergy.

The 4-Way Synergy: Mediterranean-style plant nutrition, progressive resistance & Zone 2 exercise, intensive cognitive training, and strict vascular risk regulation reduce cognitive decline by up to 30%.
