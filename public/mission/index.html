The Science of Lifespan

Six pillars of healthy longevity.

Modern geroscience demonstrates that aging is driven by interconnected biological processes. Translating this evidence into daily practice centers around six foundational domains.

PILLAR 01 🧬

Cellular & Molecular Biology

Targeting the biological hallmarks of aging: cellular senescence, autophagy clearance, DNA repair efficiency, and telomere integrity to preserve tissue function.

Senolytics Autophagy Mitochondria
PILLAR 02 🥗

Metabolic & Nutritional Health

Harnessing nutrient-sensing pathways (mTOR, AMPK, sirtuins) through circadian-aligned nutrition, glycemic regulation, and polyphenol-dense whole foods.

Insulin Sensitivity mTOR/AMPK Fasting
PILLAR 03 🏃

Physical Resilience & Exercise

Building functional capacity via Zone 2 cardiovascular endurance, progressive resistance training against sarcopenia, and optimizing VO2 max for extended healthspan.

Zone 2 Resistance Training VO2 Max
PILLAR 04 🌙

Sleep & Circadian Rhythms

Prioritizing slow-wave deep sleep for brain glymphatic clearance and metabolic reset, reinforced by daylight exposure timing and circadian regularity.

Glymphatic Clearance Slow-Wave Sleep Chronobiology
PILLAR 05 📊

Biomarkers & Early Diagnostics

Tracking biological age through epigenetic methylation clocks (GrimAge, DunedinPACE), comprehensive lipid profiling (ApoB), and subclinical inflammatory panels.

Epigenetic Clocks ApoB / Lipids hs-CRP
PILLAR 06 🧠

Neuroprotection & Mental Health

Sustaining cognitive reserve through neuroplastic challenges, stress resilience (autonomic vagal regulation), purposeful engagement, and strong social connection.

Cognitive Reserve Hormesis Vagal Tone
